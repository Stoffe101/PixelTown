# PixelTown asset-pack evaluation and licensing inventory

**2026-10-11 | Evaluated from seven user-supplied ZIPs.** This is an asset-production recommendation, **not** user approval of the final rendered style. The source-of-truth uploads stay outside public Git. A detailed **2,?**-item per-file CSV was also generated as local audit evidence; the counts below exclude most macOS metadata and Godot cache files.

## Recommendation and composition

**Primary visual language:** Pixel-boy / AAA **Ninja Adventure (CC0)**, restricted to neutral 16×16 exterior terrain, natural props, useful interiors/production furnishings, stone details, and possibly a limited set of creatures. Do **not** blanket-import its Japanese-style houses, ninja protagonists, or every biome/effect. The dense palette and rounded clusters create a better starting point than our sparse geometry, with consistent compatible objects already present.

**Caves:** Prefer a cohesive Ninja Adventure dark stone/dungeon subset first. **0x72 DungeonTileset II (CC0)** has excellent 16×16 dungeon architecture, doors, torches, traps, animated creatures and weapon icons, but has darker outlines, more restrained saturation, and different humanoid proportions. Use it as an *isolated A/B cave trial* or selectively adapt **small stone props** with palette/outline matching before introducing it into the same scene. Do not silently combine both render systems.

**Alternative, not mixed:** Ansimuz **Tiny RPG Fantasy/Town (CC0)** has attractive richer medieval architecture, 16×16 tiles, complete town and cave scenes, a PSD source for buildings and an Aseprite Dark Knight master. Its narrow palette, sharp material texture, tall slim trees, and SNES-style shading differ visibly from the lush simplified Ninja Adventure shapes. It could lead a *different coherent whole-game style*, but taking a building from one and foliage from the other would currently clash.

**Original PixelTown work remains necessary:** a recognizably medieval home/forge/storage with consistent footprint pieces, base villager and guard templates, independently aligned visible equipment and animation, a construction ghost/stages, cave entry and original faction-agnostic HUD. No candidate pack independently provides exactly these combinations.

### License-source evidence, verified

- Pixel-boy + AAA: https://pixel-boy.itch.io/ninja-adventure-asset-pack and the uploaded `LICENSE.txt`/ `README.md` explicitly establish CC0. Official example project source: https://github.com/pixel-boy/NinjaAdventure . Original project textures only, never wholesale source copy.
- 0x72: https://0x72.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii expressly declares CC0; the uploaded README describes grid/offsets but omits license text. Creator's page controls.
- Ansimuz: https://ansimuz.itch.io/tiny-rpg-town with **free-pack CC0** permission; uploaded `public-license.pdf` states free commercial redistribution. Do **not** infer the same permission for *paid* add-ons.
- Pixel Frog: https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords says current free pack may be used in commercial games but must not be redistributed/repackaged even when modified. Its separate `TS_old version_CC0 Licensed` is not what the user uploaded.
- Cainos: https://cainos.itch.io/pixel-art-top-down-basic says commercial use allowed, modification allowed, redistribution prohibited; its Unity Asset Store page has Standard EULA.
- Cup Nooble: https://cupnooble.itch.io/sprout-lands-asset-pack distinguishes **non-commercial free** from **commercial premium**; uploaded `read_me.txt` confirms free license and required attribution.

**Public Git policy:** PixelTown original artwork/code remains All Rights Reserved. CC0 third-party artwork stays identified by creator and license even if committed in future; never relabel it as proprietary authored art. External packs with a no-redistribution clause must never be committed as public raw assets. Music/fonts/sounds were inventoried but **not selected or cleared for use** here.

## Art-quality compatibility summary

| Pair | Compatibility | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Ninja Adventure nature + its neutral props/stone | **High** | Same 16px grid, chunky soft-corner outlines, similar saturation and shading |
| Ninja Adventure village + its dungeon modules | **High** | Same internal palette, layout and sprite idiom; still requires authored cave composition |
| Ninja Adventure + 0x72 small cave props | **Conditional** | Both 16px; 0x72 is darker, more densely outlined and taller for walls/NPCs; adjust palette/outline and bottom offsets |
| Ninja Adventure + Ansimuz town structures | **Low-medium** | Comparable grid but Ansimuz is much more sharply textured/detailed, with tall roof silhouettes |
| Tiny Swords + Ninja or Ansimuz | **Low** | 64px world grid and large RTS units/buildings vs 16px village scale |
| Cainos + Ninja | **Low** | 32px vs 16px base and different olive palette and stair/wall perspective |
| Sprout Lands + Ninja | **Low** | Pastel softness/minimal faces and incompatible license; not an option |

## Aseprite production-art v1 workflow for two creators

**Recommended provisional grid:** 16×16 terrain, base character canvas **24×32** with two centered silhouettes for comparison: **16×24** and **20×30**. The old 24×32 full-size silhouette remains a *comparison*, not an approved production size. Ninja Adventure's native 16px human sprites show that the larger candidate risks dominating its 16px plants and walls. Decide at normal in-game zoom. **Do not change canonical art-size decision D-028 until actual side-by-side capture passes review**.

- `art/source/characters/base-villager.aseprite`: layers `shadow / body-skin / hair / tunic / arms / legs / equipment-behind / equipment-front`; all 4 directions have stable feet/pivot `(12,31)` on a 24×32 cell.
- Use a single Aseprite timeline per shared character master, tags `idle`, `walk` (4 frames), `work` (2-4), `carry`, `attack`, `hit`. Equipment overlays export on **identical canvas, frame and anchor**, making headgear/sword/shield additive and not variant-specific.
- Create `source/terrain/grass_paths.aseprite` on 16×16 cells with grass middle/edges/corners, dirt edges (3×3 minimum), planted soil, shore variants. Palettes saved as Aseprite .ase/.gpl; don't import a game screenshot as a palette.
- `source/architecture/medieval_modules.aseprite` with reusable layered stone plinth, timber frame, warm plaster, doors/windows, roof slopes, chimney and forge-station props. Building footprints are multiples of 16 and larger front facades can overhang with transparent height.
- Put lighting on separate, optional Godot layers (amber forge/torch tint, cold cave tint), never blur original PNG textures. Outline generally 1px in coherent dark colored family, 2–3-color ramp per main material, brightest highlight primarily northwest/top-left, darker material edge on lower-right. Avoid pure black heavy silhouettes and mixed source-game pixel densities.
- Export deterministic non-trimmed PNG strips with 1:1 pixel cells and consistent file names, `SpriteFrames` or imported `Sprite2D` region rectangles in Godot. Nearest sampling and integer pixel scaling; y-sort using bottom-foot origin rather than mid-sprite.
- Use Git history for changes, keep `assets/third_party/PROVENANCE.md` + SHA256 manifest, never overwrite a purchased pack's source file by hand. Author-produced .aseprite masters belong to PixelTown's proprietary part, CC0 selections remain third-party dependencies.
- After the first art sign-off, lock palette ranges, shadow direction, door opening proportion, helmet overlays and sheet-naming conventions. Show before/after screenshots at 1080p and **3440×1440** for every substantial scale change.

## Initial integration scope and hold points

Start with **Ninja Adventure's CC0 natural/environmental textures from its pinned official Godot V4 sample**, keeping the original movement/carrying/construction demo code and guard equipment until our Aseprite masters improve. Extract *only a shortlist* to a separate `assets/third_party/ninja_adventure/` directory with reproducible SHA256 checks. Build/CI scripts may copy them from a user-supplied original ZIP or upstream pinned checkout. Full pack import is **not** approved.
