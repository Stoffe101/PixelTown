## Contributions

Please do not submit unsolicited code, artwork, music, sprites, models, fonts, sound effects or other copyrightable materials. Opening an issue to discuss gameplay or report bugs does not give permission to reuse our project's original code or assets.

If the owner later invites contributions, the contributor and owner must **agree on the applicable rights and terms in writing before** merging contributed copyrighted material. Merely opening a pull request does **not** transfer ownership or waive the contributor's copyright. The repository-level ARR notice does not magically transfer rights from other authors.

## External dependencies and artwork

No third-party asset or code may be committed without:
- documented author, source URL and original license;
- explicit confirmation of compatibility with a proprietary game (including commercial distribution if applicable);
- any attribution, redistribution and source-offer duties preserved;
- clear separation between the external component's license and the original game's ARR material.

Do not copy sprites or screenshots from existing games, use unlicensed music, scrape proprietary sprite sheets, or upload secrets or purchased closed assets to this public repository.
